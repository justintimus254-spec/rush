Rush privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version

Rush does not collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (which shift you're on, stars, career stats and shift history) and your voice setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your own headset or computer. They never leave the device. Clearing the app's data deletes them.
  • The head chef's and line cooks' voices are made by your device's built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded and the game never uses your microphone.
  • In VR, the game reads your headset and controller positions to draw the scene. That data is used live on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Network requests

The game files are downloaded from the site that hosts Rush. The fonts load from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request (see Google's privacy policy). After the first launch, the game can run offline from its cache.

Children

Rush collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
