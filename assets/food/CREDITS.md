# Scanned food models

All four models are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are CC0 (public domain): free for any use, including commercial, with no attribution required. Textures were downscaled from 1k to 512 px for the Quest, and each model is packed into one glTF JSON file (mesh and textures embedded).

| Folder | Poly Haven asset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| hamburger_buns | https://polyhaven.com/a/hamburger_buns | buns on Wagyu Smash Burger plates |
| lemon | https://polyhaven.com/a/lemon | lemon bowls in the dry store and the bar |
| food_lime_01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/food_lime_01 | lime bowl at the bar |
| yellow_onion | https://polyhaven.com/a/yellow_onion | onion tray in the dry store |
